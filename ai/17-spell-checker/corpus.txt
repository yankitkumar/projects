The weather was beautiful on the morning of the trip, and the whole family was ready to leave the house early. We packed the car with food and water because the road to the lake is long, and the children wanted to stop and play along the way. My brother drove while my sister read the map, and they argued about the best road to take, but in the end they agreed that the old road was the better one.

I definitely believe that a good trip needs a good plan. We had a separate bag for each person, and a small bag for the snacks. Before we left, my mother said that we would receive a call from the hotel if the room was not ready, and she asked us to keep the phone close. The hotel is near the water, and the view from the room is beautiful in the evening.

After lunch the weather changed and the sky turned grey. The wind was cold and the rain started to fall, so we stayed in the car and listened to music until it stopped. When the sun came out again, the lake looked bright and the air smelled clean. The children ran down to the water and the rest of us followed them, and we spent the afternoon walking along the shore and talking about the week ahead.

In the evening we cooked dinner together. There was bread and cheese, soup and fruit, and the children ate more than anyone expected. It was a simple meal, but everyone agreed that it was the best part of the day. We talked about school, about work, and about the next trip, and we decided that it would be a good idea to travel more often because the time together matters more than the place.

The next morning we woke early and the weather was fine again. We had breakfast, packed the bags and said goodbye to the lake. On the way home the children slept in the back of the car, and the road seemed shorter than it had been the day before. I think the whole family will remember the trip for a long time, and I believe we will go back to the same place next year.
